What research says, and what it does not

We do not scare. We show what is known, with sources and limits.

71 studies

Attention, stress and sleep

A meta-analysis in Psychological Bulletin (about 98,000 participants) links heavy short-video use with poorer attention and impulse control, and with more stress, anxiety and poorer sleep.

What this does not prove: it is a correlation. It is not known whether the videos cause it, or whether people who struggle more reach for them.

Source: Business Standard on the meta-analysis
5 h 36 min

Time online

According to the NASK report "Nastolatki 3.0" (Teens 3.0, 2023), teenagers in Poland spend on average 5 hours 36 minutes online per day, and about one in four (25.6%) finds it hard to think about anything other than social media.

What this does not prove: the report covers the internet in general, not short videos alone.

Source: NASK, Nastolatki 3.0 (gov.pl, in Polish)
about 4 min

How fast the algorithm works

In a test by NASK and Dyżurnet.pl, a new TikTok account registered as a teenager received "patostream"-type content (videos of harmful behaviour streamed for attention) after about four minutes.

What this does not prove: the test concerns TikTok, not YouTube Shorts, and the full methodology was not published.

Source: money.pl on the NASK test (in Polish)

What you can do today

1

Install No Shorts

The Chrome extension hides Shorts in search, on the home page, in the menu and on channels. Links to Shorts open like normal videos.

On a child's Family Link profile, install from the Chrome Web Store: the child taps "Ask a parent" and you approve with your password. Keep the Family Link setting "Extensions" turned off. A child can still remove the extension without a password.

Add from Chrome Web Store
2

Set the Shorts limit in Family Link

Extensions do not exist on phones, but YouTube has a built-in daily Shorts limit. Zero minutes means blocked. We tested it on the account of a child under 13. It only works when the child uses their own Google account everywhere.

See the step-by-step guide
3

Talk

A conversation works better than any tool. To start, ask:

  • "What made you laugh online lately?"
  • "Do you ever watch for longer than you mean to?"
  • "What would you change if you could?"

Privacy as a rule, not an add-on

Tools for children have to be trustworthy by design.

Zero data

No Shorts does not collect, store or send any data. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

Open source

Anyone can check what the extension does. The project is free and MIT licensed.

Honest limits

It works on a computer. A child with full control of the profile can turn it off. It does not replace conversation or supervision.
