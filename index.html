No Shorts

A Chrome extension that hides YouTube Shorts: from search, the home page, the menu and channels. Links to Shorts open as normal videos. It collects no data and needs no special permissions.

How we work

Tools for children have to be trustworthy by design.

Transparency

Open code under the MIT license. Decisions and plans are public. If a claim has a source, we link it, and we say what it does not prove.

Community first

Parents, teachers and developers shape the tools. Contributions are welcome, and the project stays independent of any company.

Safety and education

We teach, we do not scare. A tool never replaces a conversation with your child, and we are honest about what each tool cannot do.

Security by design

No data collection, minimum permissions, no remote code. Dependencies are kept few and updated.
